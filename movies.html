Top 5 Movies & Playlist

My top five movies

  1. The Lion King (1994)

    I enjoy how the music complements Simba’s journey back home.

  2. Finding Nemo (2003)

    I like the underwater setting and the humour that carries the story.

  3. WALL·E (2008)

    I admire how the film tells much of its story with very little dialogue.

  4. Hidden Figures (2016)

    I like how it shows the persistence and teamwork behind the space programme.

  5. Soul (2020)

    I appreciate its playful animation and its focus on finding joy in ordinary moments.
